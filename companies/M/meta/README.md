Every question tagged to Meta is listed, most-asked first, split across pages of 800. The **Why It Is Listed** column says how each question reaches Meta: tagged to the company itself, asked at companies in the same business domain, or commonly asked across its tier. Role profiles with few direct tags are filled from those pools up to 60 questions, ranked by how many employers ask the question — the same order Locus uses when a filter is thin.
