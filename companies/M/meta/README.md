Click a question to open it in the Locus section on SkillMeet, which shows that question and its answer. **Sign in to SkillMeet first** — the link runs the Locus lookup on your account.

Every question tagged to Meta is listed, most-asked first, split across pages of 800. Each list says how many of its questions are tagged to Meta itself; a role profile with fewer than 60 direct tags is topped up from companies in the same business domain and then from the same tier, ranked by how many employers ask the question — the same order Locus uses when a filter is thin.

Where a role profile lists more than 200 questions it also has a **must-do list** of 50: the ones to work through first, best-evidenced at Meta and most widely asked.
