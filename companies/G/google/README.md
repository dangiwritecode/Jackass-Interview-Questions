Every question tagged to Google is listed, most-asked first, split across pages of 800. Each list says how many of its questions are tagged to Google itself; a thin list is topped up from companies in the same business domain and then from the same tier, ranked by how many employers ask the question — the same order Locus uses when a filter is thin. The top-up floor is 60 for a technical list and 250 for the coding list.

Where a role profile lists more than 200 questions it also has a **must-do list** of 50: the ones to work through first, best-evidenced at Google and most widely asked.
