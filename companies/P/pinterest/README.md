# Pinterest

**Tier:** Tier 2  |  **Business domain:** Social  |  **Questions listed:** 1,330  |  **Tagged directly to Pinterest:** 1,189

Every question links into the Locus section on SkillMeet, which opens that question and its answer. **Sign in to SkillMeet first** — the link runs the Locus lookup on your account.

Every question tagged to Pinterest is listed, most-asked first, split across pages of 800. The **Why It Is Listed** column says how each question reaches Pinterest: tagged to the company itself, asked at companies in the same business domain, or commonly asked across its tier. Role profiles with few direct tags are filled from those pools up to 60 questions, ranked by how many employers ask the question — the same order Locus uses when a filter is thin.

Where a role profile lists more than 200 questions it also has a **must-do list** of 50: the ones to work through first, best-evidenced at Pinterest and most widely asked.
